Delete your YIYO account

YIYO · Coded Partners (Pty) Ltd

You can permanently delete your YIYO account directly inside the app.

Delete inside YIYO

Open YIYO → Profile → Delete account and follow the confirmation steps.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have access to YIYO, you can request deletion by emailing YIYO Support.

Where possible, send the request from the email address associated with your YIYO account so that we can verify ownership more easily.

Use the subject: Delete my YIYO account

Request account deletion

What will be deleted

  • Your YIYO account profile.
  • Your Firebase authentication account.
  • Your reserved YIYO username.
  • Applicable promoter or venue-management role memberships.
  • Your Hype and Going event interactions.
  • Direct account identifiers associated with retained community contributions.

Information that may remain

Some community vibe reports may remain in anonymised form so that aggregated venue information and community history are not unnecessarily corrupted.

Moderation records may also remain in anonymised form where reasonably necessary to preserve moderation evidence, investigate abuse or protect the service.

Published event history may remain after account deletion, but identifying organiser account information is removed. Pending events belonging to a deleted organiser may be cancelled.

Certain information may also be retained where reasonably necessary to comply with law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, or establish or defend legal claims.

Verification

YIYO may need to verify that the person requesting deletion controls the relevant account before completing an email-based deletion request.

We may ask for limited information necessary to identify the account and confirm the request. Do not send your password.
